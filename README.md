# CivicEye – AI-Powered Civic Issue Reporting & Management Platform

**CivicEye** is an AI-powered civic engagement platform designed to bridge the gap between citizens and local authorities. It enables people to report public issues such as potholes, garbage accumulation, damaged roads, broken streetlights, water leakage, and other infrastructure problems quickly and efficiently.

Using AI-powered analysis, CivicEye helps identify, categorize, and prioritize reported issues, making civic complaint management smarter and more organized.

### Key Features

- **Easy Issue Reporting:** Citizens can submit complaints with descriptions, images, and location details.
- **AI-Powered Analysis:** Uses Google's Gemini AI to analyze reports, identify the type of civic issue, and generate relevant insights.
- **Automated Issue Classification:** Categorizes complaints to simplify management and routing.
- **Smart Prioritization:** Helps identify urgent problems that require faster attention.
- **Location-Based Reporting:** Connects reported issues with their geographical locations.
- **Real-Time Tracking:** Enables users to monitor the progress of their submitted complaints.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Provides an organized interface for viewing, managing, and updating reported civic issues.
- **n8n Workflow Automation:** Integrates automated workflows to streamline report processing and notifications.

### Problem It Solves

Traditional civic complaint systems can be slow, fragmented, and difficult to track. Citizens often struggle to report problems, while authorities face challenges organizing complaints and identifying urgent issues.

CivicEye addresses these challenges by combining AI analysis, structured reporting, and workflow automation into a unified platform.

### Vision

To build smarter, more responsive communities by empowering citizens with technology and helping authorities make data-driven decisions.
